Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What I collect

When you send an enquiry, I receive the details you choose to enter: your name, email address, what you're interested in, information about your business and project, and optionally your business name, phone number and current website.

How I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if we work together, deliver your website. I don't add you to a mailing list or send marketing, and I don't sell your details.

Who else handles it

The form is delivered to my email inbox by FormSubmit, a form-delivery service. Your enquiry passes through FormSubmit's systems and arrives in my Gmail account (Google). These services process the information on my behalf under their own privacy terms. If you'd rather not use the form, you can email paxlumeweb@gmail.com instead.

How long it's kept

FormSubmit says it keeps form submissions for up to 30 days. I keep enquiry emails only for as long as I need them to reply and to manage any project that follows, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent by emailing paxlumeweb@gmail.com. Please don't send passwords or payment details through the form.
