Common questions

Who is this for?

Small local businesses and sole traders, such as lawn care, cleaning, detailing, trades and tutoring, that need a simple, professional website.

How long does it take?

A Starter website is typically ready in 10–14 business days after we've received your content (services, contact details, any logo or photos) and we've agreed on the scope. More complex projects can take longer. We'll confirm a timeline in writing before any work starts.

Who owns the website?

You do. Wherever possible your site is hosted in an account in your name, so you keep control of it. We help with the setup and publishing. Optional extras like a custom domain or paid hosting are paid by you directly to the provider.

Do I need a domain name?

No. Your site can be published on a free web address. If you'd like your own domain (like yourbusiness.com.au), you buy it yourself and we can connect it for you as an optional extra ($50).

Can I make changes later?

One round of revisions is included before launch. Changes after that can be quoted separately.

How do I pay?

Nothing until you've seen a free example of your page and decided to go ahead. Then it's by bank transfer, either $250 in one payment once you've accepted the written quote and terms, or in two parts: $125 on acceptance and $125 once you've approved the final preview, before your site goes live. Bank details are only supplied privately on your invoice, and we'll never email you changed bank details. Nothing is paid through this website.

Is the example really free?

Yes. We build an example layout of your page from what you tell us and send you a private link. If you don't want to go ahead, you owe nothing and we won't keep contacting you. The example isn't published anywhere.

Can you build an online shop or booking system?

Not in the $250 starter package. If you need more, get in touch and we can talk about what's realistic.

Are the example sites real businesses?

No. GreenEdge, ShineWorks and BrightPath are fictional businesses created to demonstrate design work. Paxlume is a new studio and doesn't claim past client results.
