Refund policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

If you change your mind or something goes wrong, here's what happens to the money you've paid. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

If you cancel before work starts

You get a full refund of anything you've paid.

If you cancel before you've seen a preview

You get your deposit back, less a fair amount for work already done, which is never more than $60.

If you cancel after a preview has been sent

The $125 deposit covers the work done so far and isn't refunded. You don't pay the second $125, and the site isn't published. You owe nothing more.

If I can't finish your website

You get a full refund of everything you've paid.

If something isn't right

If your site has a fault or doesn't match what we agreed in your quote, tell me and I'll fix it. If there's a major problem that can't be fixed, you're entitled to a refund under the Australian Consumer Law.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid by bank transfer to the account the payment came from, within 10 business days of being agreed. To ask about a refund or cancel, email paxlumeweb@gmail.com.
